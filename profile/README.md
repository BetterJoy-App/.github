## 🚀 Overview
BetterJoy for Windows bridges the gap between Nintendo Switch controllers and PC games. It connects your Joy-Cons or Pro Controller and presents them to Windows as a standard Xbox controller, so games recognize them right away. You get working gyro and motion controls, rumble feedback, and the option to combine two Joy-Cons into a single gamepad. Buttons can be remapped and sticks calibrated for a comfortable fit. Developed openly on GitHub, this BetterJoy download gives Switch controller fans an easy, free way to play on a Windows PC.

## ✨ Features
- **Switch controller support** — Use Joy-Cons and the Pro Controller on your PC.
- **Xbox emulation** — Appear to games as a standard Xbox controller for instant compatibility.
- **Gyro and motion** — Enable motion aiming and tilt controls where games support them.
- **Rumble feedback** — Feel vibration through your Joy-Cons or Pro Controller.
- **Combine two Joy-Cons** — Merge a left and right Joy-Con into one gamepad.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Open BetterJoy on your PC.
2. Pair your Joy-Con or Pro Controller over Bluetooth, or connect it by USB.
3. Wait for BetterJoy to detect the controller.
4. Enable gyro, rumble, or Joy-Con combining as you like.
5. Launch your game and play with your Switch controller.
